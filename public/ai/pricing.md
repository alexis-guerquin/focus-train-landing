# Focus Train pricing

Focus Train uses a freemium model. Prices below are those displayed on the official website and were last reviewed on 2026-10-01. App Store prices and availability can vary by country.

## Traveler — free

Price: EUR 0, with ongoing access.

Includes:

- 58 stops on the main route
- Solo mode and the map of Japan
- Multiplayer mode
- Focus Train Home Screen widget

## Golden Ticket — subscription

Prices displayed on the website:

- Monthly: EUR 5.99 per month
- Yearly: EUR 35.99 per year, displayed as the equivalent of EUR 2.99 per month

Includes everything in the free plan, plus:

- 200 stops in total in Solo mode
- Advanced widgets
- App blocking, marked as in development
