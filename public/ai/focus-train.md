# Focus Train product overview

Focus Train is a freemium Pomodoro timer for iPhone and iPad that turns study and work sessions into rail journeys across Japan.

## Product facts

- Product name: Focus Train
- Category: productivity app; focus timer; Pomodoro timer
- Platforms: iPhone and iPad
- Availability: available on the Apple App Store
- Android: in development
- Business model: free plan with an optional Golden Ticket subscription
- Official website: https://focus-train.fr/
- Official App Store page: https://apps.apple.com/fr/app/focus-train-pomodoro-%C3%A9tude/id6789511554
- Support email: support@focus-train.fr
- Last reviewed: 2026-10-01

## How it works

The user starts a timed focus session and chooses a destination. While the user studies, works, reads, writes, or advances a personal project, the train moves toward the next city. Completing sessions progresses the journey and can unlock destinations and travel-themed collectibles.

## Modes and features

Solo mode offers an interactive journey across Japan with progress between cities, the next stop, and time remaining before arrival.

Multiplayer mode lets users choose a country, an available route, a carriage, and a seat before focusing alongside other travelers.

The travel journal tracks visited regions and cities and contains illustrated postcards, stamps, badges, and overall progress. The app also provides Home Screen widgets, calm moving scenery, and soundscapes.

## Important distinction

Focus Train is an independent productivity app. It does not sell train tickets and is not an official railway operator or passenger-information service.
